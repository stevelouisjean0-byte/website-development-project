LOBBYING

Lobbying & political activity

Lobbying is conducted primarily through the 501(c)(4), with registration and reporting under the Lobbying Disclosure Act as applicable. The 501(c)(3) may engage in limited, permissible education and issue analysis but not partisan or candidate-related activity. The organization does not endorse candidates for public office. Any candidate-related activity would occur only through a properly formed federal PAC, in full compliance with campaign-finance law.

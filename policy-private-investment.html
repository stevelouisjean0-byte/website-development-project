TIER 1 · HIGHEST PRIORITY

Private Investment

A credible destination for responsible capital

ISSUE SUMMARY

Making Haiti a credible, welcoming destination for responsible American capital.

WHY IT MATTERS

Private investment brings jobs, technology, and durable partnerships that outlast any single program. Clear rules, risk-sharing tools, and reliable information are what move capital from interest to commitment.

OUR ROLE
·Produce an annual investment-climate assessment.·Connect U.S. investors with vetted opportunities and local partners.·Advocate for development-finance tools that de-risk responsible investment.
POLICY OBJECTIVES
Strengthen the U.S. International Development Finance Corporation’s Haiti engagement.Improve transparency and predictability for investors.Mobilize diaspora capital alongside institutional investment.

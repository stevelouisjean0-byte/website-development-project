LEADERSHIP & GOVERNANCE

Designed for credibility, continuity, and control

The parent organization is directed by a Board of Directors, advised by a distinguished Advisory Council, and administered by a professional executive team — with deliberate overlap that preserves strategic alignment while respecting the legal firewalls between entities.

Board of Directors

9–15 members serving staggered three-year terms, balanced across business, policy, academia, and community leadership — reflecting the bipartisan character of the organization. The Board sets strategy, approves budgets, hires and evaluates the CEO, and safeguards integrity and compliance.

Advisory Council

A non-fiduciary council of respected former officials, scholars, business leaders, and community figures who lend credibility, open doors, and provide subject-matter counsel.

Executive Leadership

A President & CEO leads day-to-day operations, supported over time by senior staff for government relations, policy and research, communications, development, membership, and finance.

The founding board, advisory council, and executive team are being assembled with a deliberate commitment to bipartisan balance and expertise across business, policy, academia, and community leadership. Directors and leadership are introduced here as appointments are confirmed.

A NATIONAL ADVOCACY INSTITUTION IN FORMATION

Building a Stronger U.S.–Haiti Partnership

We bring together policymakers, Haitian Americans, business leaders, investors, scholars, and civic organizations to advance informed policy, responsible investment, democratic institutions, and long-term partnership between the United States and Haiti.

Become a Founding Member View Policy Priorities
Headquartered in New York · Government relations in Washington, D.C.
OUR MISSION

To strengthen the economic, political, democratic, cultural, and strategic partnership between the United States and Haiti by advancing informed public policy, responsible investment, democratic institutions, civic leadership, and lawful, bipartisan advocacy.

Read our mission & vision →
WHY WE MATTER

Turning community strength into policy influence

More than a million Haitian Americans contribute across American medicine, business, the arts, public service, and the academy — yet no enduring, professionally run national organization has been the credible, bipartisan voice on the U.S.–Haiti relationship. We exist to change that.

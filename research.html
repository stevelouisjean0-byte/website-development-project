RESEARCH & POLICY INSTITUTE

Credibility begins with research

Independent, rigorous, non-partisan analysis — transparent about methods, reviewed for quality, and clearly separated from advocacy. Housed principally in the planned 501(c)(3) policy institute.

HOW RESEARCH DRIVES CREDIBILITY

Research designed to be cited by people who disagree

Independent research is the foundation of the organization’s credibility. It informs our advocacy and serves the wider policy community — with the line between analysis and recommendation always drawn explicitly.

RESEARCH STANDARDS

Standards that make the work trustworthy

Independence

Research is insulated from advocacy and from funders’ interests.

Transparency

Methods, data, and limitations are stated plainly in every publication.

Peer review

Work is reviewed by the Policy Advisory Council before release.

Non-partisanship

Findings follow the evidence, wherever it leads.
